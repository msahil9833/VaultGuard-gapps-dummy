What is COC?

Certificate of Compliance (COC) is a comprehensive document that demonstrates your vehicle's compliance with AIS-189 cybersecurity standards.

COC contains 10 sections:

  1. Vehicle Information - Basic vehicle details and specifications
  2. CSMS Policy - Cybersecurity Management System documentation
  3. TARA Results - Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment outcomes
  4. Security Controls - Implemented security measures
  5. Testing Evidence - Penetration test and vulnerability scan results
  6. Incident Response - Incident handling procedures
  7. Vulnerability Management - Ongoing vulnerability handling process
  8. Supply Chain Security - Third-party security assessments
  9. Evidence Links - References to all uploaded evidence
  10. Audit Trail - Complete history of all changes (auto-generated)
